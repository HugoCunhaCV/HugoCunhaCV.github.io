cover letter

I am an IT and security professional with over twenty years of responsibility for the security, governance and availability of production systems, and I am applying for roles in Cyber Security, Information Security, GRC and Security Operations. I hold the CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701), the ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC), the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate, Qualys VMDR and Vulnerability Management Foundations, and the Research Data Management & FAIR Principles certificate; I sit the PECB ISO 27001 Lead Implementer examination in September 2026.

As a Freelancer, for the companies Axtion Games, MicroDual and 100Loops I am the single point of ownership for information security and compliance: I author and maintain the policy set (access control, acceptable use, secure development, backup and retention, incident response) mapped to ISO 27001 Annex A and NIST CSF; I own the asset inventory and the risk register; I run recurring risk assessments with likelihood and impact scoring, agree treatment plans with the business owners, and report residual risk to management in language they can act on. Alongside that governance work I implement the controls myself — hardening, least-privilege access, patch and vulnerability management, WAF and TLS configuration, tested backup and disaster recovery — and I handle third-party review and GDPR data-handling records for player and customer data.

At MTM my remit as Network Engineer covered both design and control: LAN/WAN, firewalls, VPN tunnels and network segmentation, but equally change and configuration governance — documented baselines, change requests with rollback plans, periodic firewall rule and privileged-account reviews. Earlier, at Microsoft Cambridge and Apple Cambridge, I worked inside a structured secure SDLC with mandatory code review and strict confidentiality controls on pre-release data; at Virgin Media U.K. I triaged incidents under SLA in a high-volume operation and escalated fraud, phishing and account-takeover cases.

On the operational side, my blue-team practice is current: I completed the SOC Level 1 learning path on TryHackMe — Splunk, Elastic Stack, MITRE ATT&CK, alert triage, alert reporting and SOC simulator — and I am preparing the Splunk Core Certified User and CISSP. My Research Data Management & FAIR Principles training underpins the governance work directly: data classification, lifecycle and metadata standards, IS policy development, and evidence that stands up to audit against ISO 27001 and NIST CSF.

My differentiators are analytical rigour, the ability to translate technical risk for non-technical decision-makers, and languages — Portuguese, English and Spanish (fluent), French and German (intermediate). My ambition is explicit: to grow into a Head of Cyber Security remit, owning strategy, risk and compliance end to end.

I am available immediately for an interview and would be glad to walk through my risk register, policy set and lab writeups in detail.

Kind regards,
Hugo Cunha
